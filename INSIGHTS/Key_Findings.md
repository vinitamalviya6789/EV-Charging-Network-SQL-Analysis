# Key Business Findings

## 1. Total Revenue
The EV charging network generated a total collected revenue of **₹13,261.50** from completed payments.

## 2. Total Energy Consumption
Completed charging sessions consumed a total of **884.10 kWh** of energy.

## 3. Highest-Activity City
**Jaipur** recorded the highest number of completed charging sessions, with **8 sessions**.

This indicates that Jaipur was the most active city in the current dataset.

## 4. Most Used Payment Method
**Card** was the most frequently used successful payment method, with **11 completed payments**.

## 5. Highest Energy-Consuming Vehicle Model
The **Hyundai Ioniq 5** recorded the highest total energy consumption at **134.70 kWh**.

This suggests that this vehicle model accounted for relatively high charging demand in the dataset.
